The tesseract

Each slider turns it in one plane, in radians. The last three turn it into w.

The slice keeps only the points where w = h.

The net is the boundary cut open into 8 cubes. Folding hinges them back into the tesseract. It turns only in xy, xz and yz.

M = Rzw Ryw Rxw Ryz Rxz Rxy: xy turns first, zw last.

Four corners, marked in the shadow: where each starts, and where M sends it. A and B differ in x, A and C in w, and D is opposite A.
